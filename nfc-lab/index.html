The Lab

See inside a tag

The Lab is for people who want to see inside a tag. Anything that changes a tag shows what it will write and asks first; details not yet checked on real tags are marked Unverified in the app.

🗺

Memory Map

Every page of an NTAG or Ultralight tag, colour coded by what it is for, with a risk rating. Edit a page by hand behind warnings, a confirmation and a read-back check.

⌨️

Raw Command Console

Send your own commands to NTAG and ISO 15693 tags. Every command is rated before it goes out, dangerous ones need a second confirmation, answers are explained in plain language, and you can share a transcript.

ISO 15693 Inspector

System information, a block viewer and lock status for ICODE-style tags. Read only.

🛡

Originality Check

Tests the NXP signature on NTAG213, NTAG215 and NTAG216. Honest limits: the built-in key is not yet confirmed against an NXP source, so results are worded carefully, and a pass does not prove a tag is not a clone.

📚

Chip Reference

Offline cheat sheets for common NTAG, Ultralight and ICODE chips, with anything unverified marked.

📸

Snapshots, Diff and Restore

Save a complete copy of a tag's memory, compare any two snapshots or a snapshot and a tag page by page, and restore one onto a tag of the same type after a dry-run of exactly what will change.

Set or remove a password on an NTAG213, 215 or 216, turn on the read counter, and write dynamic URLs that carry the tag's UID and a read counter. Risky settings need you to type I UNDERSTAND. The bit positions are marked Unverified until tested on real tags.

📶

Tap-Quality Test

Reads a tag repeatedly, gives a verdict and ranks the spots on your phone from best to worst.

🔎

Decode Bench

Paste hex, Base64, an NDEF message, a vCard or a Wi-Fi QR string and see what it means, offline.

📓

Notebook and Reports

Keep notes about your tags and share a tag report as Markdown, text or PDF.

Honest limits

What iPhones can't do

iOS can read and write NDEF on most tags, and reach raw memory on NTAG and MIFARE Ultralight tags. It cannot read MIFARE Classic, the chip in many hotel keys, gym fobs and older transit cards, and no iPhone app can, whatever its store listing says. NFC Lab tells you when it meets one instead of failing silently. It does not clone cards, change a tag's UID, emulate a tag or sniff other readers, and it does not work with every tag.
