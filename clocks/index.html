Mac Menu Bar App · Coming Soon

World clocks in your menu bar

A tiny world clock for macOS. Add the cities you care about, see each one's live local time, and keep your favorite in the menu bar — no accounts, no network, no tracking.

See Features Support
Capabilities

Every city you care about.
One glance away.

Requires macOS 14 or later on an Apple Silicon Mac.

🕐

Menu-Bar Clock for Any City

Show your local time or any city you choose in the menu bar, with an optional city or country name beside it. Click a row to switch.

🌗

Analog & Digital Faces

Each city gets a dial that is cream by day and indigo at night, a digital time pill, or both.

📅

Yesterday, Today, Tomorrow

Know at a glance whether it is already tomorrow where your colleague is.

🏷

Zone Abbreviations

EST, JST, CEST and friends, so it is clear which time you are reading.

🔎

500+ Searchable Places

A city picker built from the system time zone database, with real country names.

✏️

Custom Labels

Give any clock your own label, like “Bondi HQ, Sydney”. It replaces the city text in the row and the menu bar.

↕️

Drag to Reorder

Tap Edit, then drag rows into the order you want. Remove a clock with the minus button.

📏

Resizable List

Drag the handle to make the list taller or shorter. Clocks remembers your size.

⚙️

Your Format

12 or 24 hour time, with optional seconds, date and day of the week.

🚀

Launch at Login

Optional. Turn it on in Preferences and Clocks is always there when you log in.

🔒

Private by Design

No accounts, no analytics, and no network access at all. Your cities and settings stay on your Mac.
