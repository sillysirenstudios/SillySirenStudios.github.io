Keep your Mac awake,

A one-click menu bar toggle that holds your Mac awake — and checks that macOS is really doing it. No more leaving caffeinate -dims running in a Terminal window.

See Features Get in Touch
Capabilities

Awake when you need it,
asleep when you don't

Holds the same four system wake assertions as the caffeinate tool: display on, no idle system sleep, disk kept spinning, and no full sleep on AC power.

👆

One-Click Toggle

Click the mascot in your menu bar, or press ⌘K. It's full color when awake and dims when sleep is allowed.

✅

Live Verification

Checks the real macOS power management state every five seconds, so "Assertions: held" reflects what the OS is actually doing — not just what you clicked.

⏱

Timed Sessions

15 minutes to 8 hours, or a custom length, with a live countdown.

🔋

Battery Smart

Allow sleep on battery, or release automatically below a low-battery cutoff you choose.

🤖

Auto-Activate

Stay awake automatically while chosen apps are running — a render, a build, or a presentation.

⌨️

Global Hotkey

Optional ⌃⌥⌘K shortcut to toggle from anywhere. You can also let the screen saver and display sleep while the Mac stays awake.

🚀

Launch at Login

Start awake, so your Mac is ready after every reboot. No Dock icon, no clutter — it lives entirely in your menu bar.

🔒

Zero Network, Zero Data

No network access, no accounts, no analytics. Sandboxed and private.
