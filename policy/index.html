Privacy Policy

All Silly Siren Studios apps  ·  Last updated: October 2026
Short version: None of our apps collect personal data. They have no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking of any kind. Nothing is sent to Silly Siren Studios.

All Apps

This policy covers Terminal, Insomnia, and Leaflet. No account is required to use any of them. We do not collect, store, or transmit personal data, and we do not know how many people use our apps, how often, or what they do with them.

The apps contain no analytics, crash reporting, advertising SDKs, or tracking of any kind. None of them are designed for or directed at children, and none collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Terminal

Data. Server credentials and SSH keys are stored locally on your device and never leave it except as part of direct SSH connections to servers you configure.

Network. The app connects only to SSH servers that you explicitly add and connect to. All network traffic goes directly from your device to your server. No data is routed through Silly Siren Studios servers or any third-party infrastructure.

In-app purchases. Purchase transactions are handled entirely by Apple via StoreKit. Silly Siren Studios does not receive or store your payment information. We have no visibility into who purchases Pro beyond what Apple provides in aggregate App Store reporting.

Insomnia

Network. Insomnia never connects to the internet. It has no servers, no accounts, and nothing to sync. Everything it does happens entirely on your Mac, using Apple's own IOKit power management APIs.

Data. The only thing Insomnia keeps is whether you've registered it as a Login Item, which macOS itself manages via SMAppService — the same mechanism any app uses for "Open at Login." Nothing else is written to disk, and nothing is measured, logged, or transmitted about how you use the app.

Leaflet

Network. Leaflet has no network entitlement — it is not just configured to avoid network use, it is sandboxed in a way that makes network access technically impossible for the app. It cannot phone home, check for updates, load remote images, or fetch anything from the internet.

Data. Leaflet only reads the specific Markdown files you choose to open, and never writes to or modifies them.
